# Evidence for retained weights

The current roster retains six active models for useful size/strength comparisons. Apprentice, Pacer and Refined Nano were archived after reviewing the supplied complete All controllers benchmark `2710b642c3a123a5be9d6ec897182ec0892c7150baa39d84db1f82c20c470433`: 1,056 games, eight paired seeds, twelve controllers. They scored 14.5, 30 and 27 league points respectively out of 88; this small local sample supports roster curation, not universal strength claims. No new performance study was run during cleanup. Exact model identities, retirement flags and archived source paths are in [catalog.json](catalog.json).

- **Robust Strong:** the preselected `follow-strong-league-6211` model gains 3.63 rally-score percentage points over the prior full-size winner on the six-reference confirmation benchmark; direct games are 337 wins, 169 losses and 6 draws. It remains below 50% rally score against Attacker and Defender. Original evidence: `training/results/robustness-lab/README.md`, `evaluation/confirmation.json`, `evaluation/verification.json`, `releases/manifest.json` inside the archive.
- **Plain Nano:** the preselected `precise-cma-7151` model has 447 parameters and 408 MACs. Its original confirmation gives 332 wins, 145 losses and 35 draws against unchanged Pacer, but Defender remains a weakness. Original evidence: `training/results/plain-nano/README.md` and its `evaluation/` and `releases/` directories inside the archive.
- **Refined Nano (archived):** trained `6→16→3` Tanh, 163 parameters and 144 MACs. The frozen `cma-fine-g40` candidate improves its unchanged starting model's six-opponent rally score from 26.62% to 30.46% (+3.84 points, paired-seed 95% CI +3.04 to +4.61); direct games are 264 wins, 202 losses and 46 draws. Plain Nano remains stronger. A separate fresh comparison gives Refined Nano 30.02% versus Quill Plus 48.90% on the same six-opponent field; direct games are 46 wins, 434 losses and 32 draws. Refined Nano scores better against Attacker, the exception to Quill's advantage. Each study uses 256 paired seeds, both sides and five rallies per game. Frozen identities, protocols, checkpoint, outcomes and runtime verification: `public/training/refined-nano-v4-evidence.zip` and `/training/refined-nano-v4.html`.
- **Quill Nano (retired baseline):** handwritten `6→12→4→3→3` ReLU weights, 163 parameters and 141 MACs, without training or automated fitting. After manual development on 32 seeds, the frozen candidate's 128-seed native ONNX test gives 782/448/50 rally wins/losses/draws against Pacer, 670/554/56 against Robust Strong, 882/375/23 against Plain Nano and 778/464/38 against Vector Nano. It loses to Prism Small, Attacker and Defender. Both side assignments and five rallies per side are included. All ten matchup outcomes, hashes, development records and runtime verification are in `public/training/quill-nano-v4-evidence.zip`; the report is `/training/quill-nano-v4.html`. The original bytes are retained at `tests/fixtures/models/quill-nano-v4.onnx`; its constructor in `scripts/quill_dense_v4.py` supplies Quill Plus's base arrays.
- **Quill Plus:** four handwritten scalar changes, with the same 163-parameter network. On a new matched 128-seed test, the ten-reference mean rally score increases from original Quill's 54.93% to 57.53% (+2.60 points, seed-bootstrap 95% CI +1.98 to +3.22). Pacer improves from 782/459/39 to 869/330/81 rally W/L/D; Defender improves from 26/1038/216 to 100/858/322. Plain Nano and Vector Nano regress. Plus versus original Quill is 562/603/115, with no demonstrated head-to-head gain. Source: `scripts/quill_plus_dense_v4.py`; full frozen evidence: `public/training/quill-plus-v4-evidence.zip` and `/training/quill-plus-v4.html`. No training or automated fitting was used.
- **Pacer / Apprentice (archived):** learned `6→128→128→3` Tanh DAgger exports from the gradual-speed study. Their original selection manifests and candidates are under `training/results/pace-study/training/` inside the archive. Their unchanged ONNX files are retained in `tests/fixtures/models/` and `public/training/retired-v4-models.zip`. Pacer remains a training/evaluation reference; Prism Small and Quill Plus are now the default app matchup.
- **Vector Nano:** fixed dense `6→28→9→3` ReLU weights, 487 parameters and 447 MACs. Original evidence: `training/results/vector-dense-v4/` inside the archive. Construction is maintained in `scripts/vector_dense_v4.py`.
- **Prism Small:** fixed dense `6→42→33→3` ReLU weights, 1,815 parameters and 1,737 MACs. Original evidence: `training/results/robust-dense-v4/` inside the archive. Construction is maintained in `scripts/robust_dense_v4.py`.
- **Tracker:** a 163-parameter dense baseline retained for simple matches and device testing, not a strength claim.

Robust Small's reported gains used a shortcut architecture rejected by the current validator. It is archived, not promoted. Old dense models are retired for roster relevance, not declared ineligible merely because of their age. Historical comparisons used different reference fields; their aggregate scores must not be compared as if they were one leaderboard.
