Unchanged rules · legal alternatives · controlled learning test

Result: legal placement improvements exist, but the trained continuation is not an overall upgrade. It improves against the defender and regresses against the attacker.

First, test whether a strong script leaves a better reachable shot available. Then test whether a plain neural network learns those choices and improves against opponents in complete matches. These are separate claims.

Change the labels, hold the budget fixed

Both arms start from the identical Pacer weights: 6 → 128 → 128 → 3, 17,795 learned parameters. Both see the same pooled observations, minibatches and optimization budget. The control uses the original adaptive attacker's labels; treatment substitutes successful branch actions on the oversampled tactical rows. No trajectory calculations or scripted decisions are embedded in either export.

The first 24-round attempt regressed in both arms. A single follow-up reduced the learning rate and tactical-row frequency and anchored outputs to the original Pacer. Treatment selected round 44 of 48; control selected round 0, so its primary export is unchanged Pacer. The trained control at round 48 is a secondary diagnostic. Checkpoint selection uses development performance against the attacker and defender. Loss and development curves below are not final-test results. A winning action path is only one way to reach its contact; exact action imitation is not the same as learning useful placement.

Imitation accuracy is not tactical success

On fresh probe seeds, the model controls the receiving paddle until its next contact. The original scripts then resume, isolating the placement choice. The opportunity count includes only positions where the scripted baseline loses and a legal alternative wins.

The trained network agrees with more action labels, but finds one fewer winning placement than unchanged Pacer. There are often several valid action paths to a good contact, and a label sequence is optimized for the scripted continuation. A network playing the rest of the rally can make different choices.

Fresh seeds do not guarantee unseen states: these scripted trajectories often converge. State-overlap and separate seen/unseen probe summaries are retained in the measurements. These correlated probes are not independent match trials.

The scripts leave exploitable choices; this training method did not produce an overall stronger player. The selected treatment improves against the defender but regresses clearly against the attacker. Its head-to-head difference against Pacer is uncertain. The arena keeps its existing models.

Reproduce the test

The retained experiment contains the complete action prefixes and every candidate branch, shared training data, raw logs, selected checkpoints, source hashes and fresh evaluation seeds. The independent scalar checker verifies the legal transitions and terminal outcomes. Published final seeds are now consumed; future selection requires new seeds.

.venv/bin/python training/scripts/check_classic_tactics.py
.venv/bin/python training/scripts/report_classic_tactics.py

Exact discovery, training, freezing and evaluation commands are in training/results/classic-tactics/README.md. A failed continuation is evidence about this training method and budget, not a proof that a network cannot beat the scripts. This experiment does not test human enjoyment or change the game rules.
