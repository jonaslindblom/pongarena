Make movement
a decision.

Playable preview · separate rules and rankings

Ordinary movement stays available. A short burst gives the paddle extra reach, then its visible energy meter must recover. The ball accelerates half as quickly as Classic v4. This preview tests whether placement and resource use can create longer, understandable exchanges.

Pressure, then a counter

The attacking script repeatedly sends the ball to the top. The defender reaches it but spends energy each cycle. Once its meter is nearly empty, the attacker switches to the bottom and scores. The second replay changes one return: the defender spends more energy to contact at Y=19 instead of Y=18. That changes the shot angle and breaks the pattern; the same controllers then reach a loop draw.

Actual diagnostic replay: seed 2,828,560,875, rally 1. Both panes share the recorded prefix. The alternative uses an exactly reachable 26-action path, then resumes the original policies. These are geometric diagnostic controllers, not trained weights.

A small, explicit rule addition

  • Normal up/down: 2 units per tick. Burst up/down: 4 units if at least 8 energy is available.
  • Each paddle starts with 96 energy. A successful burst costs 8; every normal action restores 1, including normal movement. An exhausted burst request moves normally but does not recharge.
  • Every return increases horizontal speed by 0.5, from 5 to a cap of 13. Placement sets the angle. The cap arrives after 16 returns, around 21 seconds into an uninterrupted rally.
  • Models see eight inputs: the original six plus both energy meters. Five outputs choose up, stay, down, burst up or burst down. Energy belongs in the exact loop-state key.

The 96/8 budget was retained for the preview because it gives a 12-tick full burst and longer pressured exchanges than the shorter tested budgets. No physical-phone or subjective human-playtest result is claimed.

Reproduce and train independently

The downloadable package contains standalone TypeScript and Python engines, a headless example, and exact action fixtures. Checks cover 12,000 independent scalar/vector transitions, 102 isolated examples and three full matches; TypeScript and Python agree on 11,905 full-match frames. Models trained for Classic v4 are not compatible with this eight-input/five-output contract.

python3 training/burst/check.py
node --import tsx training/burst/check.ts
.venv/bin/python training/scripts/burst_check.py
.venv/bin/python training/scripts/burst_tactics.py
python3 training/scripts/burst_tactic_check.py
.venv/bin/python training/scripts/burst_study.py --confirmation --count 32 --out /tmp/new-burst-confirmation.json
python3 training/scripts/package_burst.py
python3 training/scripts/burst_report.py
