PONGARENA / HANDWRITTEN REFINEMENT

Four weight changes.
A broader improvement.

Still 163 parameters · No training · 128 fresh paired seeds

Quill Plus keeps the original 6→12→4→3→3 ReLU network and 141 MACs. Four manually calculated scalar changes improve its long-flight forecast and recovery. Across ten unchanged reference opponents, the mean rally score rises from 54.93% to 57.53% on common held-out serves.

There are tradeoffs. Plus improves against several stronger opponents, but regresses against Plain Nano and Vector Nano. Its direct matchup with original Quill shows no clear advantage. Quill Plus is the retained active model, selected for its stronger ten-opponent average; original Quill remains in the archived evidence and test fixtures.

The aggregate improvement is +2.60 percentage points, with a seed-bootstrap 95% interval of +1.98 to +3.22. Each of 20,000 bootstrap samples resamples whole seeds jointly across both models, all ten opponents, both sides and all rallies. The positive aggregate interval was the promotion criterion fixed before final evaluation. This is a fixed opponent-field result, not a complete league or a universal strength ranking.

All inference used the actual frozen ONNX files, native CPU execution with batch one, deterministic first-maximum selection and exact-loop detection under unchanged Classic v4 rules. These seeds are different from both original Quill's development/confirmation seeds and this refinement's 64 development seeds. They are now consumed.

Directly against original Quill

Quill Plus records 562 wins, 603 losses and 115 draws, or 48.40% rally score. Paired contests are 45 wins, 59 losses and 24 draws. The 95% point-margin interval is −0.0734 to +0.0110, spanning zero. The experiment does not demonstrate a head-to-head improvement.

Plus also continues to lose to Prism Small, Attacker and Defender, despite reducing those losses. Original Quill had stronger measured matchups against the other Nano presets; that tradeoff is retained in the report even though Plus is the sole active Quill preset.

What the four scalars change

Two square-curve weights: the forecast's last segment starts at 4/3 but legal long flights can reach 17/6. A closed-form minimax secant extension balances positive and negative approximation error over that interval. The two corresponding positive/negative weights change together. Near-contact forecast features remain unchanged.

Two recovery-gate weights: while the ball travels away, the old opponent-dependent position offset now shrinks in proportion to 1 − horizontal speed / 13. At maximum speed it becomes zero, bringing the paddle to center when return coverage is tightest. Incoming aiming remains unchanged because the outgoing-velocity neuron is zero.

Every other scalar, bias, layer width and operation is identical to original Quill. There are no additional inputs, preprocessing, branches or physics helpers at inference. The constructor generates the original handwritten arrays and changes exactly four values.

Development and limits

We compared ten manually specified revisions with the unchanged baseline on 64 new development seeds. They tested exact centering, alternative square curves, Prism-inspired safe-contact aiming, long-flight correction and speed-dependent recovery. Several alternatives regressed; all retained proposals and their results are in the evidence download. Separate diagnostic policies used exact physics to identify error sources; those policies are not submitted models.

The selected speed-recovery candidate raised the six-reference development mean from 39.47% to 46.67%. It was frozen before the ten-reference confirmation test and has not been tuned on those final seeds. No gradient descent, optimizer, supervised distillation, regression, evolutionary search or automated parameter fitting was used. Manual design decisions were informed by development matches.

The network still assumes cap-speed flight time, has a coarse product approximation and only one accurate wall fold. It does not plan multiple returns. The original Quill model bytes are preserved in a regression fixture, and its constructor and frozen evidence remain unchanged.

Reproduction and validation

The final constructor reproduces the model byte-for-byte. The active validator confirms 163 parameters, 141 MACs, four Gemm layers and three ReLUs. A regression test verifies that exactly four matrix entries differ from original Quill.

Native ONNX and ONNX Runtime Web WASM agree on 35,190 decisions: 4,110 probes and eight full matches against Pacer and Robust Strong. All 15,572 frames reproduce in the live TypeScript engine, with zero action or state mismatches. Maximum score difference is 0.000009537, below the 0.00002 tolerance. All 92 automated tests pass without skips.
